# Delhi Master Plan 2041 (MPD-2041): Transport & Mobility Policy
**Publisher:** Delhi Development Authority (DDA)  
**Publication Year:** 2021 / Gazette Notification 2023  
**Coverage:** Connaught Place Heritage Commercial Precinct & Central Delhi Arterial Corridors  
**Document ID:** doc_delhi_mpd_2041  

## Section 1: Overview of the Central Commercial Zone
Connaught Place (Rajiv Chowk) represents the central economic and administrative core of New Delhi. It features a concentric ring road structure comprising the Inner Circle, Middle Circle, and Outer Circle (Connaught Circus), intersected by 12 radial arterial avenues (including Barakhamba Road, Janpath, Parliament Street, and Kasturba Gandhi Marg).

## Section 2: Traffic Volumes & Peak Demand Dynamics
- **Vehicular Intensity:** The Outer Circle carries over 90,000 equivalent passenger car units daily, operating near capacity (v/c > 0.85) between 08:30–11:00 AM and 17:30–20:30 PM.
- **Radial Arterials:** High-capacity radials such as Barakhamba Road and Janpath have multi-lane divided carriageways with design capacities up to 3,600 veh/hr per direction.
- **Pedestrianization Interventions:** Phased traffic restrictions on the Inner Circle redirect circulation to the Middle and Outer circles, necessitating coordinated signal phases at radial entry portals.

## Section 3: Signal Timing & Heritage Corridor Guidelines
- **Cycle Times:** Standard signal cycle lengths across the Connaught Circus intersections are mandated at 120–150 seconds during peak commute periods to accommodate high turning volumes.
- **Bottlenecks and Detours:** Lane closures or construction incidents along Connaught Circus result in severe spillback along radial approaches; dynamic rerouting across Janpath and KG Marg is mandated for all major infrastructure maintenance.
- **Speed Limits:** Speed limits in the commercial precinct are capped at 40 km/h on Outer Circle and 30 km/h on radial connectors to ensure pedestrian safety around transit hubs.
