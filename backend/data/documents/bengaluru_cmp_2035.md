# Bengaluru Comprehensive Mobility Plan (CMP) 2035

**Authority:** Directorate of Urban Land Transport (DULT) & Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC)  
**Corridor Focus:** Silk Board Junction – Outer Ring Road (ORR) – HSR Layout – Electronic City Flyover  
**Document Version:** 2035 Horizon Framework  
**Classification:** Municipal Transportation Policy & Infrastructure Standard  

---

## Chapter 1: Corridor Context & Silk Board Congestion Profile

Central Silk Board Junction (CSB) serves as the primary southern gateway to Bengaluru, connecting the Outer Ring Road (ORR), Hosur Road, BTM Layout, and HSR Layout. It carries in excess of 165,000 passenger car units (PCU) per standard weekday, making it one of the highest-volume at-grade junctions in South Asia.

### Key Traffic Characteristics
- **Peak Hour Volume:** Morning peak hours (08:30 to 11:30 AM) and evening peak hours (17:30 to 21:00 PM) observe link volume-to-capacity (V/C) ratios exceeding 1.85 on approaches to the junction.
- **Average Peak Speeds:** Traffic speeds on the Outer Ring Road approaches drop to 8–12 km/h during peak demand periods.
- **Modal Split:** Two-wheelers and private cars constitute 64% of total vehicular trips, with city buses (BMTC) carrying 31% of total commuters despite comprising only 4.5% of the vehicular stream.

## Chapter 2: Transit Infrastructure & Interventions

### Phase 2A & 2B Metro Alignment (ORR-Airport Line)
- The Silk Board interchange station functions as an integrated multi-modal transit hub connecting the Yellow Line (RV Road – Bommasandra) with the Blue Line (Central Silk Board – KR Puram – Kempegowda International Airport).
- Grade-separated bus priority loops are planned to bypass at-grade turning conflicts between Hosur Road and the Outer Ring Road.

### Bus Priority Lanes & Dedicated Right-of-Way
- A 12 km dedicated bus lane along the Outer Ring Road from Silk Board to KR Puram is designed to enhance transit throughput from 3,200 passengers per hour per direction (PPHPD) to 12,000 PPHPD.
- Strict enforcement of lane discipline is projected to reduce bus travel times along the corridor by 35%.

## Chapter 3: Signal Timing & Traffic Management Protocols

### Adaptive Traffic Signal Control
- Implementation of automated adaptive traffic signals using modified Webster optimum cycle calculations.
- Recommended cycle times during peak demand: 180 to 210 seconds, with green splits dynamically apportioned based on upstream queue detector telemetry along Hosur Road and the ORR flyover approach.
- Inter-stage amber clearance is fixed at 4.0 seconds with an all-red clearance interval of 2.0 seconds to safely clear broad multi-lane approaches.

---

## Chapter 4: Freight Restrictions & Incident Bottleneck Management

### Commercial Vehicle Entry Timings
- Heavy Commercial Vehicles (HCV) and medium goods carriers are prohibited from traversing the Silk Board junction between 07:00 and 11:00 AM, and between 16:00 and 22:00 PM.
- Dedicated incident management vehicles and rapid-clearance tow trucks are stationed at HSR 5th Main and BTM 29th Main to minimize shockwave queue formation during vehicular breakdowns.
