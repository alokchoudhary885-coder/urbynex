# Jaipur Comprehensive Mobility Plan (CMP) 2025
**Publisher:** Jaipur Development Authority (JDA) & Urban Mass Transit Company (UMTC)  
**Publication Year:** 2019 / Revised 2024  
**Coverage:** Jaipur Walled City and South-North Arterial Corridors (MI Road - JLN Marg)  
**Document ID:** doc_jaipur_cmp_2025  

## Chapter 1: Executive Summary & Urban Growth
Jaipur's urban agglomeration has expanded significantly, resulting in elevated vehicular travel demand along radial arterial corridors. Key transit spines connecting the historic Pink City (Walled City) to the southern educational and commercial hubs include the Mirza Ismail (MI) Road and Jawaharlal Nehru (JLN) Marg.

## Chapter 2: Modal Split & Flow Characteristics
- **Two-Wheelers & Private Cars:** Represent approximately 68% of motorized trips within the central business area.
- **Peak Hour Factor (PHF):** Morning peak hours occur between 09:00 and 11:30 AM, with peak flow factors reaching 1.35 to 1.45 relative to average daytime hourly volume.
- **Evening Peak:** Evening peak extends from 18:00 to 20:30 PM, characterized by retail-bound traffic along MI Road and Ajmeri Gate.

## Chapter 3: Speed Regulations & Capacity Design Standards
- **Arterial Roads (MI Road, JLN Marg):** Design speed is 50 km/h, with daytime operating speeds averaging 28-35 km/h due to frequent intersections and commercial curb activity.
- **Secondary / Radial Links:** Baseline design speed is 40 km/h; effective capacity ranges from 1,200 to 1,800 passenger car units (PCU) per lane per hour under unconstrained conditions.
- **Signalized Intersections:** Control delay at major junctions (such as Statue Circle, Government Hostel Crossing, and Ajmeri Gate) accounts for up to 45% of total corridor travel time during peak periods.

## Chapter 4: Traffic Management & Planned Interventions
1. **Corridor Protection:** Closure or traffic calming of minor collector streets is recommended during festival and VIP transit windows to prevent gridlock.
2. **Signal Coordination:** Implementing Webster-optimized cycle lengths between 90s and 120s can reduce intersection queue delay by 18-24%.
3. **Emergency Bottleneck Management:** In the event of carriageway obstruction or construction on MI Road, secondary detour paths via Ashok Marg must maintain minimum clear widths of 7.0 meters.
